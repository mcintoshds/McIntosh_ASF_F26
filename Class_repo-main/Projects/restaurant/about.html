Our Story

Independent kitchens · Shared tables

The Underdog began beneath an old boiler shop in Gearford, where steampunk bulldogs govern the streets above. A small crew of corgi cooks made a place where neighbors could choose their own supper.

Today, humans, corgis, and other animal travelers gather around the same tables. Airship crews stop in after a shift, engineers trade stories over tea, and couriers rest their boots by the green lamp.

Good Food, Quiet Company

Our kitchen serves breakfast, lunch, and dinner with alcohol-free house tonics. Expect warm plates, seasonal ingredients, and a welcome that does not depend on a ration card.

Find the Green Lamp

Look behind the old boiler shop in Lower Gearford. We serve Tuesday through Sunday, 8:00 a.m.–11:00 p.m., with the last reservation at 10:00 p.m.
